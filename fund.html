The Story Behind the Fund

Some mothers are carrying responsibilities that are bigger than what the community can see.

In Namere Community, many single mothers are doing everything possible to keep their children safe, fed, educated, and loved. For a mother raising a child with special needs, the journey can be even heavier. She may need extra money for medical check-ups, transport, learning materials, nutrition, therapy referrals, school follow-up, and daily care. At the same time, she still has to look for rent, food, school fees, and income for the whole household.

These mothers are not weak. They are strong, patient, and full of love. But strength alone is not enough when a child needs special attention and the mother has no steady income, no proper support system, and limited access to professional guidance.

SAMI Foundation was born from this reality. Our founder, Sarah Mirembe Namusisi, understands the journey of single motherhood deeply. Through determination, love, and faith, she raised her children and supported them through education. Her story became the foundation of a bigger mission: to stand with single mothers, especially those caring for children who need extra care, patience, and protection.

Why Special Needs Support Matters

Every child deserves to be seen, supported, and included.

Many children with special needs are misunderstood because their development, speech, learning, movement, behaviour, or health needs may be different. Some are hidden at home because families feel ashamed or overwhelmed. Some miss school because the family cannot afford transport, learning materials, medical reviews, or a supportive learning environment.

SAMI Foundation wants to help mothers replace fear with understanding, isolation with community, and helplessness with practical support. We want every child to be treated with dignity and every mother to know that she is not alone.

  • Support for mothers raising children with special needs and developmental challenges.
  • Guidance on patience, positive parenting, child protection, and inclusive care.
  • Help with school materials, learning support, and school follow-up where possible.
  • Transport support for clinic visits, assessments, therapy referrals, or community services where resources allow.
  • Food, hygiene, and emergency family support for the most vulnerable households.
  • Skills training and livelihood support so mothers can earn while caring for their children.
How Donations Help

Your donation creates practical support for children with special needs and their mothers.

Every contribution helps SAMI Foundation build a caring system around vulnerable families: the child, the mother, the home, the school, and the community.

Special Needs Care

Support for child-focused needs such as learning materials, clinic transport, referral follow-up, nutrition support, and caregiver guidance where resources allow.

Mother & Caregiver Support

Counselling, encouragement, parenting guidance, peer support, and safe spaces for mothers who feel overwhelmed or isolated.

Inclusive Education

School materials, education follow-up, inclusive learning encouragement, and support to keep vulnerable children connected to learning.

Emergency Family Relief

Food, hygiene support, rent pressure relief where possible, and urgent household support for families in immediate difficulty.

Livelihood Skills

Skills training, small business coaching, savings groups, and practical income activities to help mothers become more independent.

Dignity & Awareness

Community awareness that reduces stigma and helps families understand that children with special needs deserve love, patience, protection, and opportunity.

More Than Charity

This is about dignity, inclusion, and a better future for children who need extra support.

A mother caring for a child with special needs often carries emotional, financial, and physical pressure every day. When she receives support, the whole family becomes stronger. When the child receives patience, learning support, health guidance, and inclusion, the child’s future becomes brighter.

Your donation helps ensure that no mother feels forgotten and no child is pushed aside because their needs are different or because their family lacks resources.

Together, we can help single mothers build stable families, support children with special needs, and create a more caring community — one family at a time.
