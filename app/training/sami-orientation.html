SAMI Foundation Orientation & Vision

For committee members, leaders, trainers and authorised volunteers joining the SAMI Committee Hub.

Estimated study time: 1.5 hours

1. Why SAMI exists

SAMI Foundation exists to empower single mothers to build stable families and sustainable livelihoods. The Foundation’s work combines practical skills, livelihood support, education support, mentorship, savings and financial capability, and health and wellbeing.

Key principle: every programme should strengthen dignity, capability, family stability and long-term independence rather than create unnecessary dependency.

2. How the organisation works

SAMI uses a committee-led operating system with clear responsibilities across executive leadership, governance oversight, donor and sponsor relations, project coordination, finance and administration, field/secretarial work, training and mentoring, and beneficiary leadership.

Members may hold more than one authorised role, but access to information and approvals must always follow the permissions of the role being performed.

3. Professional conduct

Committee members are expected to communicate respectfully, attend and prepare for meetings, keep accurate records, meet agreed deadlines, disclose conflicts of interest, protect confidential information and separate personal interests from Foundation resources.

Never: approve your own unsupported expenditure, invent beneficiary information, share another member’s password, or disclose sensitive beneficiary information outside authorised SAMI work.

4. Safeguarding and dignity

SAMI works with families and may handle information about children and vulnerable people. Safeguarding concerns must be taken seriously, recorded factually, shared only with authorised people and escalated through the appropriate protection process. Collect only the personal information genuinely required for programme delivery.

5. Evidence, reporting and accountability

Activities, meetings, reports, expenses and training records should be supported by reliable evidence. Drafts are not the same as approved records. The Committee Hub uses workflows so that submission, review, approval and verification are separate actions.

Training completion is also subject to verification: a member can report completion and upload evidence, but official SAMI compliance requires an authorised verifier.

6. The leadership mindset

SAMI’s leadership goal is to build a working system that can continue beyond any one individual. Strong leaders create clarity, develop other leaders, document decisions, use resources responsibly, learn from evidence and build trustworthy relationships with communities, partners and donors.
