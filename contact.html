Partner With the Bigger Vision

We welcome partners for future facilities and larger family-support projects.

SAMI Foundation is seeking future partnerships with donors, sponsors, churches, companies, health professionals, teachers, therapists, counsellors, social workers, and community leaders who can help build a stronger support system for single mothers and children with special needs.

Future facility-based projects may require over €250,000 as the foundation expands into structured spaces for special needs support, inclusive learning, livelihood training, counselling, nutrition support, emergency relief storage, administration, and outreach.

Partnership areas

  • Special needs guidance and referrals.
  • Education, therapy, health, and counselling support.
  • Training equipment and livelihood tools.
  • Food, school materials, and emergency relief supplies.
  • Facility development, transport, and operational support.
